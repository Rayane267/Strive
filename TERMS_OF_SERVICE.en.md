Strive is a decision-support tool for ride-hailing drivers. The App analyzes, via OCR, the ride offers displayed by the Uber, Bolt and Heetch apps to calculate in real time the estimated profitability of each ride (hourly rate, per-kilometer rate) and helps you make an informed decision.

Strive **is not affiliated with Uber, Bolt or Heetch** and does not itself provide any passenger-matching service.
